CloudNest Backup – FAQ

CloudNest Backup is an automated backup service for small and medium-sized businesses, developed by Amber Soft.

How often are backups taken?

On the Starter plan, backups run once a day at 2:00 AM. On the Business plan, every 4 hours. On the Enterprise plan, backups are continuous, with a delay of no more than 15 minutes.

How long are backups retained?

The Starter plan keeps backups for 30 days, the Business plan for 90 days, and the Enterprise plan for 365 days. Enterprise customers can extend retention to up to 7 years on request.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored in two data centers located in the European Union. Data is encrypted with AES-256 both in transit and at rest. On the Enterprise plan, encryption keys can be managed by the customer.

How do I restore a deleted file?

Log in to the customer portal, select the device, and open the "Version history" tab. Choose the backup date and click "Restore". Individual files can also be downloaded directly in the browser. Restoring an entire server requires contacting technical support.

How do I contact technical support?

Technical support is available on business days from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM at support@cloudnest.example. Enterprise customers have access to a 24/7 emergency line with a guaranteed response time of 1 hour.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Starter and Business plans can be cancelled with one month's notice. After the contract ends, data is retained for 14 days and then permanently deleted.
