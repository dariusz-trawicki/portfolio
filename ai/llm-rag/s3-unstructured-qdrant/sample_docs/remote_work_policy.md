# Remote Work Policy – Amber Soft Ltd.

Version 2.1, effective from March 1, 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed their probation period. Employees on probation may work remotely for a maximum of 1 day per week, subject to their manager's approval.

## 2. Work Model

The company follows a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Office attendance is mandatory on Tuesdays, when team meetings and the weekly product demo take place.

Fully remote work is only possible with written approval from the department director and the HR team. Requests must be submitted through the HR system at least 30 days before the planned change.

## 3. Core Hours

Regardless of location, core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 14:00. During this time, employees are expected to respond to Slack messages within 30 minutes.

## 4. Equipment and Allowances

Every employee receives a company laptop, a 27-inch monitor and a headset. Employees working remotely at least 2 days per week receive a monthly allowance of 60 EUR to cover electricity and internet costs.

A one-time home office budget of 350 EUR is available for furniture and equipment such as a chair, desk or lamp. Invoices must be submitted within 3 months of purchase.

## 5. Security

Remote work is permitted only from within the European Union. Working from any other country requires approval from the IT Security team, requested at least 14 days in advance.

All access to company resources must go through the corporate VPN. Using public Wi-Fi networks without the VPN enabled is prohibited. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to security@ambersoft.example within 24 hours.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the HR team at hr@ambersoft.example.
